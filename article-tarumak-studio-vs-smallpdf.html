Tarumak Studio vs Smallpdf — Honest Comparison

2026-06-27 · Updated November 2026 3 min read

Smallpdf is a polished Swiss product with more than 30 document tools. Tarumak Studio is ours: free browser tools without uploads. We are biased, so what follows about Smallpdf is taken from its plan overview, privacy notice and security article, read on 24 November 2026.

See Our PDF Tools →

In two sentences

If you edit, sign, translate or convert documents every day, Smallpdf is the more complete tool and worth paying for. If you now and then merge, split, shrink or protect a file and would rather not upload it, Tarumak Studio is enough.

The free plan has a daily download limit; the overview does not name the number. Pro starts with a seven-day trial. Prices depend on country and billing period, so see the pricing page.

What happens to your file

Smallpdf processes files on servers and says that those from free tools are deleted one hour after processing, and that the servers are in Ireland. Files you save in an account stay until you delete them. It names ISO 27001 certification and compliance with European, Californian and Swiss data protection law.

Tarumak Studio reads the PDF in your browser and does not receive it. We hold no certification. The difference is simple: with one you rely on a company’s promises and controls, with the other the file is not sent.

For a flyer, this makes no difference. For somebody else’s medical report, ask first whether you are allowed to upload it. Companies should look at their own rules; an approved service with a data processing agreement can be the correct choice even if a local tool exists.

Three everyday cases

A student with lecture notes

Merging and compressing twenty files before an exam quickly reaches a daily limit. Use the PDF Merger and PDF Compressor. To turn a scanned chapter into editable text, Smallpdf’s OCR is more comfortable than our route through images.

A freelancer with contracts

Protect drafts with the PDF Password Protector without uploading. For the signature itself use a signing service: Smallpdf’s Pro plan includes one.

An office with forms and reports

Regular editing, conversion and shared access call for a paid product with support. We would not recommend our tools as the only solution there.

Weak points of both

  • Smallpdf: an internet connection and upload time, limits on free use, and the best compression only in Pro.
  • Tarumak Studio: limited by the memory of your device, no editing or signing, no support desk, and compression that works only on JPG photos inside a PDF.

Costs over a year

Count how often you need a function that only a paid plan offers. If it is twice a year, a monthly subscription for that month may be cheaper than an annual one, provided you cancel in time. If it is every week, the subscription costs less than the working time lost on detours.

Frequently asked questions

How many free tasks does Smallpdf allow?

Its plan overview mentions a daily download limit without a number. Check the current pricing page.

How long does Smallpdf keep files?

It states one hour after processing for free tools. Files saved in an account stay until you delete them.

Does Tarumak Studio have OCR?

Yes, for images: OCR Image to Text gives you plain text. It does not make a scanned PDF searchable.

Which is better for confidential files?

A tool that does not upload them avoids one risk. In a company, follow its rules on approved software.
